📖 How to Play

🐾
1. Move your character

Move your character with the arrow keys or WASD (or the on-screen D-pad on mobile).

🔮
2. Drag your purple shadow

Drag the purple shadow with your mouse or finger anywhere across the board.

🧱
3. Shadows pass through walls

Your shadow can pass through walls in the marked area. Physical walls never block it!

⭐
4. Collect stars & activate switches

Touch stars and activate switches using your shadow to trigger magical mechanisms.

🚪
5. Guide character to the exit

Once the exit opens, guide your character to it to complete the puzzle!

🔄
6. Restart anytime if stuck

Stuck? Press Restart to try again. Puzzles are relaxed with unlimited attempts.
